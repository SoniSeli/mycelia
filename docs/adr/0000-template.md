What problem are you solving, and why now? List the constraints that matter: mobile performance, testability, team size (one person), time, platform limits. Keep it factual, two to five sentences.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** what gets easier.
- **Negative:** what gets harder, or what you're accepting as a trade-off.
- **Follow-up:** work this creates, and when you'd revisit the decision (e.g. "if levels exceed 50, reconsider Addressables").
